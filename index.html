Meet Leo

The data aggregator that listens for you.

Leo is Enrichify's newest platform. It listens across social media for people talking about the brands you compete with: what they dislike, what they want instead, and where they are. Then it turns that conversation into clean, ready-to-use audience data.

Leo does not run your ads. It tells you exactly who to reach and what to say. That is the part that decides whether a campaign works.

"The ad is only as good as the audience behind it. Leo builds the audience."
