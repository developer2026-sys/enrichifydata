The process

The engine, step by step

Six steps from a single search to a measurable sale.

1

Curate active searches

We build a list of live Google searches tied to your products, services and competitors, and the organic terms worth ranking for.

2

Turn searches into prospects

Search activity is matched to real, reachable people in your markets, so you know who is shopping right now.

3

Build audiences by intent

Prospects are grouped by what they searched, so each audience gets a message written for it.

4

Serve targeted ads

Creative runs across Facebook, Instagram and display ads on the sites your prospects already visit.

5

Retarget every visitor

Paid clicks and organic visitors land on your campaign page. If they leave without acting, ads follow them on social and the web.

6

Measure and refine

Monthly reporting on reach, clicks, visits and conversions, with match-back analysis so you see exactly what worked.
