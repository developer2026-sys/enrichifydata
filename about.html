About Enrichify

We turn fragmented data into a growth advantage

Enrichify is a purpose-built platform designed to strengthen customer and donor relationships while uncovering new revenue and engagement opportunities through intelligent, data-driven insights.

Who we are

Intelligent data. Smarter growth.

By enhancing data visibility and optimizing outreach strategies, we help organizations better understand their audiences, increase engagement, and drive more predictable, sustainable growth.

Our model mimics the time-tested techniques of direct marketing — trusted by professionals for more than 70 years — and moves it online. True audience targeting gives you the power of extraordinary relevance, and the more relevant the message, the more likely the conversion.

What enrichment delivers

  • → Complete, current contact records
  • → Clear visibility into customer behavior
  • → Higher re-engagement from existing audiences
  • → Efficient targeting and less wasted spend
  • → Predictive insight into who acts next

Data Enrichment & Appending

Behavioral Intelligence

Predictive Scoring Models
